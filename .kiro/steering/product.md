# SplitEase – product

SplitEase is a MERN expense-splitting app for roommates and trips.

Core features: JWT auth, groups (add members by email), expenses with equal or custom splits,
per-member balances, "simplify debts" (fewest payments), settle-up, UPI deep-link payment buttons,
and natural-language expense entry ("dinner 1200 split with Rahul and Aman") that creates a DRAFT
the user confirms before saving.

Out of scope: receipts/images, notifications, multi-currency, email invites.
